Is your hospital bill right?

We read your bill line by line and show what may be worth asking the hospital about, with the evidence.

  • Free. No sign-up.
  • Your files are deleted after we read them.
  • A few minutes of your time, plus a short wait while we read the bill (usually under 15 minutes).

What this check is not

  • Not legal, medical or financial advice. It is information only.
  • It does not promise any outcome.
  • It does not check what insurance will pay.
  • You decide what to say to the hospital.

Your privacy, in short

  • What we collect: the documents you upload, for this check only.
  • Why: to read the bill and find things worth asking about.
  • How long: files are deleted once read; everything else when you leave, unless you choose to share at the end.
  • Who sees it: only the services we use to read your bill and run this site, under contract. They may not keep your files or use them to train their systems. We never send anything to the hospital or an insurer.
  • Your rights: you can stop and delete everything at any time.
